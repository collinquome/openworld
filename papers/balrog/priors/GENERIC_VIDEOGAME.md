# priors/GENERIC_VIDEOGAME v0.1 (provenance: prior:generic)

G1. HP reaching 0 means death; keep HP above expected burst damage.
G2. Loot is usually good; picking up items usually pays.
G3. Deeper/later content is harder; difficulty scales with progression.
G4. Bosses/eliet enemies telegraph; unusual glyphs = unusual danger.
G5. Healing/resting is available and worth time when safe.
G6. Consumables (potions/scrolls) exist to be used, especially in emergencies.
G7. Vendors/shops trade fairly; taking without paying is punished.
G8. Ranged attacks beat melee against dangerous enemies.
G9. Leveling up (XP) before hard content pays off.
G10. Stuck = look for a hidden passage/secret.
