# priors/NETHACK_SPECIFIC v0.1 (provenance: prior:nethack — pretraining knowledge, pre-wiki)

N1. Prayer rescues emergencies (hunger/low HP) but is rate-limited; wrath is real.
N2. Eating fresh corpses is the food economy; some species are poisonous/cannibal-taboo.
N3. Elbereth scares most monsters (canonical panic button).
N4. Cockatrice-class corpses/touch petrify.
N5. Floating eyes freeze on melee; never melee them.
N6. Pick-axe enables dig-descent (fastest depth strategy).
N7. Early Gnomish Mines dwarves are lethal to weak roles.
N8. Pets follow and can be swapped with; they are not obstacles.
N9. Shopkeepers/guards are peaceful but extremely dangerous when angered.
N10. Speed differences decide flee viability; you cannot outrun faster monsters.
