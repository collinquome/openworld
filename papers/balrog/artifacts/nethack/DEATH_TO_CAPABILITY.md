# Death → Capability → Strategy map
Grounded in the observed death taxonomy (all logged episodes). The 0-for-7 mean-mover drops were all DECISION/positioning/advice levers. The deaths are dominated by RESOURCE failures — largely-untried capability levers. This is the forward path: inject the missing CAPABILITY, not better decisions.
Priority = death-mass × tractability × untried-ness.

## TIER 1 — HUNGER (~145 deaths: starved 93 + fainted 52) — the #1 killer, and it's LOGISTICS not combat
Capability gap: food economy. Fainting-from-hunger (52) = pure management failure (let hunger reach Fainting with food available/securable).
Strategies/capabilities:
- **HARD ANTI-FAINT GUARD** [most tractable single fix in the program]: never let hunger reach Weak without eating any available food (safe corpse / carried ration / floor food). A rule-base HARD-GUARD. Should eliminate most of the 52 faint deaths outright.
- **Corpse-eating aggressiveness**: our safe-corpse table may be too conservative — eat every safe fresh corpse (nutrition banked). Verify the safe table isn't rejecting edible corpses.
- **Food-paced descent**: don't dive faster than food supply (the trajectory-avoidability finding — exploration burns the clock, but STARVING at depth 1-5 turns 2k-9k means we're not eating what's there).
- **Gold→food**: buy food in shops when gold is available (Tourists start with gold).
- **Prayer-for-nutrition** timing (have it; the 1000-turn economy).
Proximal KPI: hunger-death rate (starved+fainted). Target: halve it → directly ~+70 episodes' worth of survival.

## TIER 2 — TRASH MELEE (~287: jackal/bat/fox/kobold/hobbit/newt/rat/dog/pony) — attrition, die at ~35% HP
Capability gap: we're under-geared/under-leveled and LOSE the attrition (proven: fleeing doesn't work, same-speed). The fix is WIN FASTER / TAKE FEWER HITS / OUTSOURCE THE FIGHT — not flee.
Strategies/capabilities (all largely UNTRIED = the real headroom):
- **PET UTILIZATION** [zero prior use, high value]: the starting pet fights trash for free + IDs items + triggers traps safely. Keep it alive, fight alongside it, let it tank/kill trash. Direct answer to this class at zero cost. Pet-preservation + fight-with-pet doctrine.
- **WIELD UPGRADE** [zero wield actions ever]: wield the best available weapon (found long sword >> starting dagger). Higher dpt = win the fight in fewer rounds = fewer incoming hits. Character sheet's counterfactual_power already ranks weapons.
- **ARMOR (mid-game)**: wear found armor — inert early (no armor) but the AC model is built; fires on mid-game drops. Fewer hits land.
- **SAFE EARLY LEVELING**: the agent hits the D5-6 kill zone at xp 1-3. Kill weak/safe things early (with pet help, at range) to arrive stronger — trash becomes trivial at xp 5+. (Not the failed XP-pace-gate; targeted safe kills.)

## The strategic reframe
DECISION levers (flee/kite/throw/advise): EXHAUSTED, 0-for-7, deaths are capability-bound.
RESOURCE levers (food-guard / pet / wield / items / safe-XP): LARGELY UNTRIED, and they target exactly the two dominant death classes (hunger + trash-attrition). The #1 killer (hunger, 145) is the most tractable — a hard anti-faint guard.
Demonstration learning (E35c, session 9): validates WHICH of these experts actually use, and how — the honest arbiter of the priority order above.
